2. Page list

#PageRolePurpose / key content
—Landing + Role switcherAllPitch: "dorm 3,500 + Complete Living 990 = 4,490 vs condo 7,500+". Buttons to enter as each role.
T1CatalogTenant5 single items and 3 bundles, with the lead bundles first. A running monthly total with a 1,500 THB/mo cap warning.
T2Item / Bundle detailTenantExample models, price, bundle saving, what is included (maintenance, warranty).
T3CheckoutTenantPartner dorm + room number, term (6 or 12 months), delivery wave date, fee summary, contract and damage policy acceptance.
T4Order confirmationTenantWave date, what happens next, and a "LINE OA" contact note.
T5My SubscriptionTenantActive items, monthly fee, contract start/end, deposit held, and action buttons.
T6Report an issueTenantPick an item, then the issue type: malfunction (warranty) or damage (fee schedule).
T7Exit requestTenantThe 3 pathways with a live fee calculation and the 14-day notice date.
T8BuyoutTenantShown after 24 months: buy in-room items for 25–35% of retail.
T9InvoicesTenantThe room invoice with the add-on as a line item (rent, water, electricity, dorm-dorm).
T10Room takeoverTenantThe incoming tenant of a pre-upgraded room accepts the existing items. Setup fee waived.
L1DashboardLandlordUpgraded rooms, subscription revenue, and the 15% share this month.
L2RoomsLandlordStatus per room: No add-on, Active, or Pre-upgraded (vacant).
L3Room detail + Check-outLandlordInspection checklist, damage deductions from the room deposit, and "items stay in room".
L4Monthly invoicesLandlordInvoices per room that include the add-on line.
O1DashboardOperatorActive subscriptions, the next wave, open tickets, and pending exits.
O2Orders + Delivery wavesOperatorGroup new orders by dorm cluster into a wave day (e.g. Wed/Sat), then mark them delivered.
O3AssetsOperatorEach unit's status: Warehouse, Installed (room), In repair, or Retired. Also shows age and warranty.
O4Issue ticketsOperatorTriage each ticket to a warranty claim (free) or a damage fee (from the schedule).
O5Exit requestsOperatorProcess transfer, relocation, or return. Schedule pickup, settle the deposit.
O6Partner dormsOperatorDorm list, rooms, and revenue share.

3.5 Lease ends: room turnover

The default is in-situ retention: the items stay in the room for the next tenant.

flowchart TD
  A["Tenant lease ends"] --> B{"Tenant choice"}
  B -- "Buy out items, after 24+ months" --> C["Pay 25–35% of retail; items become tenant's"]
  B -- "End subscription" --> D["Landlord check-out inspection"]
  D --> E{"Damage found?"}
  E -- Yes --> F["Deduct scheduled fee from deposit"]
  E -- No --> G["Full deposit refund"]
  F --> H["Items stay in room"]
  G --> H
  H --> I["Room marked Pre-upgraded vacant"]
  I --> J["New tenant moves in"]
  J --> K{"Takes over add-ons?"}
  K -- Yes --> L["Room takeover: setup fee waived; new subscription active"]
  K -- No --> M["Operator schedules pickup; asset back to Warehouse"]

4. Shared mock data

Every page reads from one mock data set:

  • Catalog: 5 SKUs and 3 bundles, with the prices from OVERVIEW.md.
  • Partner dorms: 3–5 Rangsit dorms, each with rooms.
  • Subscriptions: tenant, room, items, term, start date, deposit, and status.
  • Assets: SKU, serial, status, room, warranty end.
  • Tickets and exit requests.

The diagrams need an internet connection, because Mermaid loads from a CDN.
